Lamborghini 350 GT

The Lamborghini 350 GT was the first production car of the Italian brand, manufactured between 1964 and 1966. Equipped with a V12 engine of 3.5 liters and 280 hp, the two-seat coupe helped Automobili Lamborghini compete directly with Ferrari and guaranteed the company's initial success.

Ferrari 250 GTO

The Ferrari 250 GTO is one of the most iconic cars in the history of the Italian brand, produced between 1962 and 1964. With a V12 engine of 3.0 liters and 300 hp, the model was designed to compete in GT races and became a symbol of exclusivity and performance. Only 36 units were manufactured, making it extremely rare and valuable.

Specifications:

  • year: 1962-1964
  • mileage: 5,000
